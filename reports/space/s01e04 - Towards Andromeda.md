# Once Upon a Time... Space — s01e04: Towards Andromeda
Pierrot and Psyche's patrol ship is caught in a "gravitational storm" near a cluster of young stars and crashes on a scorching planet. With the robot Metro they trek to cooler ground, work out where they are from the stars, and end up in the hands of rebels armed by Cassiopeia. The real science is light: navigating by star positions, a remark on young star clusters, and the number of stars in our galaxy.

## Simplified but fine
- "My memory banks classify 200 billion individual stars, and that's just in our own galaxy": within the usual range for the Milky Way (about 100–400 billion).
- Fixing position from three stars and needing darkness to see constellations is sound navigation.
- "Young stars ... if one falls into the gravitational field of another, it can unleash immense energy": vague, but close encounters and mergers of stars in dense young clusters are real.
- The catalogue numbers are used fictionally: the real NGC 7052 is an elliptical galaxy in Vulpecula, not a young star cluster.
