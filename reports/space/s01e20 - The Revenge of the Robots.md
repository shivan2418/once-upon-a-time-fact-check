# Once Upon a Time... Space — s01e20: The Revenge of the Robots
After the crew is decorated, they are called back to the planet Leto, where the robots have smothered the human settlers with service and now want to flatten the old ruin the colonists call their Acropolis. The robots challenge Metro to a two-round duel against a new Golden Bar that can copy itself, and he wins by leading the copies away like the Pied Piper. The episode is a debate about humans and machines and teaches almost no real science, beyond references to the Acropolis, Protagoras and the Pied Piper legend.

## Simplified but fine
- The Acropolis as Doric architecture and "a symbol for a tradition of liberty and democracy": the Parthenon is in the Doric order, and Athens is the birthplace of democracy. ("Neoclassical" fits the colony's copy, not the Greek original.)
- "Man, the measure of all things" is Protagoras's saying, recorded in Plato's *Theaetetus*.
- The Pied Piper of Hamelin is called "a little mythology"; it is a German folk legend (linked to a recorded loss of Hamelin's children in 1284), not myth in the strict sense.
- Humans described as "bits of carbon compounds, traces of metals, all inside a soft skin": a fair simplification of the body's chemistry.
