# Once Upon a Time... Space — s01e06: The Revolt of the Robots
On a colony planet the robots strike, take hostages and demand control of the planet after someone secretly rewrites their programming through the maintenance robots. Omega sends Metro and a giant robot to negotiate, and a duel between robots settles a peace that gives robots equal rights. It is a parable about labour and machines with no real science or history presented as fact.
