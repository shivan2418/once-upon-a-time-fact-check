# Once Upon a Time... Space — s01e25: The Battle of the Titans
General Grouchley seizes sole power on Cassiopeia and sends Omega an ultimatum, then turns on his robot allies of Yama when the Great Computer delivers its own ultimatum. The Yama fleet wipes out Cassiopeia's Nautiluses and forces Grouchley to surrender. Meanwhile Pierrot, Psyche and Metro talk their way into the flying city, Trojan-horse style, and set it to blow up. The episode is pure science fiction; the one real astronomical detail is the location of the nebula NGC 6188.

## Simplified but fine
- "Our allies are located in the nebula mgc 6188 in the southern region of Scorpio" ("mgc" is a mishearing of NGC): NGC 6188 is a real emission nebula about 4,000 light-years away. It lies in Ara, the constellation directly south of Scorpius, so "south of Scorpio" is fair for that part of the sky.
- The Trojan horse "in Greek days ... in antiquity": correctly given as an ancient Greek story (it is legend, not documented history).
