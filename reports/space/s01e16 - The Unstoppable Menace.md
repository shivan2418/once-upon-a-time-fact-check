# Once Upon a Time... Space — s01e16: The Unstoppable Menace
Cassiopeia launches a shielded robot bomb from beyond Uranus that will strike Earth within hours. The council cannot intercept it, so Pierrot and Psyche land their small ship on it near Jupiter and use their engines to push it off course, sending it into the Sun. The episode shows the order of the planets and real moons and asteroids (Rhea, Titania, Mimas, Vesta, Deimos), but makes few factual claims.

## Simplified but fine
- The bomb's route passes Uranus, Saturn, Jupiter, the asteroid belt, Mars and then Earth: the right order of the planets (the timings are fiction).
- "Sirius and Barnard" as allies near the solar system: Barnard's Star (about 6 ly) and Sirius (about 8.6 ly) are among the Sun's nearest neighbours.
- "The moon, well, it's less than two seconds from the target": light takes about 1.3 s to travel from the Moon to Earth.
- "There is no means known to propel an object faster than the speed of light": correct physics.
- Jupiter's strong radiation and radio noise disrupting communications is broadly true.
- Caligula, Nero and Hitler as examples of power-mad rulers is ordinary historical shorthand.
