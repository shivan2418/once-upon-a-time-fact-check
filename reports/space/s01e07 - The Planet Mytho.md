# Once Upon a Time... Space — s01e07: The Planet Mytho
The patrol finds the stranded crew of the ship "Olympus", a family named Jupiter, Hera, Ares, Hermes and so on. They live like gods above a primitive people and refuse to share fire or knowledge with them. The episode retells Greek myths (Peleus and Thetis, Eris's golden apple, the Sphinx's riddle, Prometheus's fire, Pandora) as a story about stranded space travellers, and Metro gives a one-line statement of Newton's law of gravitation.

## Simplified but fine
- "Each planet attracts its satellites with a force which is exactly proportional to its mass and inversely proportional to the square of the distance": Newton's law, except that the force is proportional to the product of *both* masses.
- The myths are told recognisably: Zeus's interest in Thetis and her marriage to the mortal Peleus, Eris and the golden apple, the Sphinx's riddle (answer "man"), Prometheus giving fire, and Hephaestus making Pandora as punishment.
- The episode mixes the Roman name Jupiter with Greek names (Hera, Ares, Hermes, Demeter); Demeter is correctly his sister.
