# Once Upon a Time... Space — s01e26: The Infinity of Space
The Omega Confederation gathers its fleets for a hopeless last stand against Yama. Maestro hunts for a way through the robots' force field, and Pierrot plans a suicide raid. Mysterious beings tell the fleet to steer clear of the unstable "Howard Star", which then explodes and sweeps away the robot fleet, and they urge humanity to grow in spirit. The real science is limited to a nod to John Wheeler's wormholes and to Einstein, and a guess that the blast was a supernova or a black hole.

## Simplified but fine
- "The theory of dynamic geometry topological wormholes ... that old outdated theory of Wheeler": John Wheeler's geometrodynamics ("dynamic geometry") and his "wormholes" (he coined the term in 1957) are real. Metro's claim that they move bodies from point to point overstates it: Fuller and Wheeler (1962) showed such wormholes pinch off too fast for anything to cross. Traversable wormholes were proposed later (Morris and Thorne, 1988) and need exotic matter, so Maestro's dismissal is reasonable.
- "Howard Star ... It's unstable" followed by "Was it a supernova? Black hole?": the star's name is probably a mishearing. That an unstable massive star can blow up as a supernova is real science, though the scene is fiction.
- "There are in the cosmos forces ... dreamed of in our philosophies" echoes Hamlet ("more things in heaven and earth ... than are dreamt of in your philosophy").
