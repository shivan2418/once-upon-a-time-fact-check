# Once Upon a Time... Space — s01e05: The Cro-Magnons
Mercenaries are hired to invade a planet whose people live like Ice Age hunters. Omega's computer goes through candidate worlds, giving real data about galaxies and star clusters (M101, M13, M33, M31), and settles on the planet Diane in Andromeda. The patrol helps a Cro-Magnon-style tribe and its old sorcerer, who tells of ancient "sky beings" who taught his ancestors, and the patrol fights off the invaders.

## Wrong (even in 1982)
- **Quote:** "Nersi, small planet on the outer edge of spherical star cluster M13 in the constellation of Hercules. Cluster comprises 300,000 stars and has a diameter of 23 minutes of arc or 25,000 light years."
  **Today:** About 23 arcminutes is right for M13's apparent size, but its real diameter is only about 150–170 light years. About 25,000 light years is roughly its *distance* from us (current estimate about 22,000–25,000 ly), so the script has mixed up distance and diameter. The star count of a few hundred thousand is fine.
  **Confidence:** high
  **Severity:** minor
- **Quote:** "Plato spoke about an alien civilization brought to Earth people by gods from outer space."
  **Today:** Plato's *Timaeus* and *Critias* tell of Atlantis, founded by the sea god Poseidon. Nothing in them involves outer space or aliens. Reading Plato this way comes from 1960s–70s "ancient astronaut" books (von Däniken and others), not from the texts.
  **Confidence:** high
  **Severity:** major

## Simplified but fine
- M101 "in the constellation of the Great Bear ... 23,377,400 light years": the right constellation, and the distance matches the 1970s Sandage–Tammann value (7.2 Mpc). Modern values are about 21–22.5 million ly. The 8-digit precision is fake.
- M33 "in the constellation of the triangle ... 2 million light years": the right constellation. The distance was usually given as about 2.3 million ly then and about 2.7–3 million ly today.
- Andromeda galaxy M31, "400 billion stars ... 2 million light years": a fair 1980s round figure (about 2.2 million ly then, about 2.5 million ly today). Star-count estimates still range from a few hundred billion to about a trillion.
- NGC 752 is a real open star cluster in Andromeda.
- "In the Cro-Magnon days, the total population was about what it is in one city": estimates for the Upper Palaeolithic world are a few hundred thousand to a few million people.
- Ice Age hunters living in caves and rock shelters, hunting with spears and firing clay figures in a kiln. Fired-clay figurines such as the Dolní Věstonice Venus date back about 26,000 years.
