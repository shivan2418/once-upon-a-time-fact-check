# Once Upon a Time... Space — s01e19: The Strange Return to Omega
Pierrot, Psyche and Metro catch the liner Cosmopolitan home to Omega and find its new officers acting oddly. A cargo ship blows up after a confused distress call, and Metro discovers that several officers are humanoid robots that have replaced the real crew. The heroes unmask them at the farewell masked ball. The episode contains almost no real science, apart from passing mentions of the asteroid belt, Jupiter and weightlessness.

## Simplified but fine
- A ship leaving Earth "passing through the asteroid belt" and then nearing Jupiter: the right order going outward. The belt's real asteroids are far apart, so a "splendid view" of many at once is artistic licence.
