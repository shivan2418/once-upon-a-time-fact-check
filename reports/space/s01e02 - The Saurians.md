# Once Upon a Time... Space — s01e02: The Saurians
After a suspicious breakdown near the planet Lago, which General Grouchley had reported as lifeless, Pierrot leads a reconnaissance mission with Maestro's new android Metro. They find a Triassic-style jungle and a civilisation of intelligent, telepathic reptiles that play a tail-bashing ball game, keep their numbers down by eating surplus eggs, and once exterminated their crocodile-like cousins. The real science is brief: the distance to the Andromeda galaxy, Triassic plants and climate, and where crocodiles, dinosaurs and pterosaurs came from.

## Common belief at the time
- **Quote:** "Descendants from the Saltoparkas, originating as crocodiles, dinosaurs, and winged reptiles."
  **Today:** "Saltoparkas" is probably a mishearing of *Saltoposuchus*, or a blend of it with *Euparkeria*, the two "thecodonts" that older books showed as the common ancestor of crocodiles, dinosaurs and pterosaurs. *Saltoposuchus* (about 215 million years ago) is now classed as an early crocodylomorph on the crocodile branch, too late and on the wrong branch to be anyone's ancestor. *Euparkeria* sits near the base of the group but is not a direct ancestor. Cladistic studies from the mid-1980s on dropped "Thecodontia" as a real group.
  **Why it was believed:** In popular dinosaur books of the 1960s and 70s, small running thecodonts such as *Saltoposuchus* and *Euparkeria* were the standard picture of the "ancestor of the ruling reptiles".
  **Confidence:** medium

## Simplified but fine
- Andromeda "at a distance of two million light years", seen "as it was" when the light left: 1982 references gave about 2.2 million; today's figure is about 2.5 million.
- "Vegetation exactly conforms to that of the Triassic period, 200 million years ago": the Triassic ran from about 252 to 201 million years ago (1982 tables said roughly 225–190).
- "The atmosphere is rich in carbon dioxide. It accelerates the pulse.": breathing extra CO2 does speed up breathing and heart rate. Triassic air did hold several times today's CO2.
