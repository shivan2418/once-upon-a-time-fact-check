# Once Upon a Time... Space — s01e23: City in Flight
Pierrot and Metro slip onto the robot planet Yama, disguised among a robot patrol, to rescue Psyche from the castle where the humanoids are trying to learn the secret of her telepathy. Omega's new warship, carrying the Colonel and Maestro, heads for the nebula NGC 6188, and the episode ends as the robots launch "Operation D.Va" and the city prepares to move. Almost everything is science fiction; the only real-science content is a couple of passing remarks about laughter and about how the brain stores memories.

## Common belief at the time
- **Quote:** "Laughter is the one thing that makes humans different from all other creatures in the universe."
  **Today:** Darwin (1872) already described laughter-like panting in chimpanzees and other apes during tickling and play, and van Hooff (1972) traced the human laugh back to the primate "play face" with its panting vocalisation. Later work found play vocalisations in all the great apes (Davila-Ross and colleagues, 2009) and ultrasonic "laughter" in tickled rats (Panksepp, from 1998). Laughter is not unique to humans.
  **Why it was believed:** "Man is the only animal that laughs" goes back to Aristotle and was a stock saying in popular books.
  **Confidence:** medium
  **Severity:** minor
- **Quote:** "Knowledge is stored using crystals in large carbon-based molecules."
  **Today:** Memory is stored mainly in the strength and pattern of synaptic connections between neurons, not as information coded into particular molecules. The "memory molecule" idea (Hydén's RNA work, McConnell's planarian experiments in the 1960s, Ungar's "scotophobin" in 1972) did not hold up when others tried to repeat it, and specialists had dropped it by the late 1970s. "Crystals" may be a mishearing or a loose translation.
  **Why it was believed:** The memory-transfer experiments got a great deal of press in the 1960s and early 1970s and lived on in popular science books.
  **Confidence:** medium
  **Severity:** minor

## Simplified but fine
- "Nebula 6188" and "the region of Scorpio": NGC 6188 is a real emission nebula. It lies in Ara, the constellation just south of Scorpius (see s01e25).
