# Once Upon a Time... Space — s01e01: The Planet Omega
An unknown, very fast alien ship keeps crossing space watched from Omega, the headquarters of a confederation of worlds. The council splits between attacking it and making contact, and a patrol cruiser that tries to make contact is easily outrun. The only real science is a short classroom scene where Maestro defines the light year, the speed of light and the parsec, and mentions Einstein's relativity and FitzGerald contraction.

## Simplified but fine
- Speed of light "300,000 kilometers per second" (exactly 299,792 km/s), and the half-finished sum "around 18 million" is right for kilometres per minute.
- Light year "9,460,800,000,000 kilometers" is exactly 300,000 km/s × 365 days, a correct rounded figure (precise value 9.4607 × 10^12 km).
- "A parsec is, as you know, 3.26 light years" is correct.
- Einstein's curved spacetime and FitzGerald (Lorentz–FitzGerald) length contraction are real physics, only name-checked here.
