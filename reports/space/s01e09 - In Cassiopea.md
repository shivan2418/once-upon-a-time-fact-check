# Once Upon a Time... Space — s01e09: In Cassiopea
General Grouchy rallies Cassiopeia against the confederation, partly by showing a satirical film of an overcrowded, polluted, car-mad and over-armed Earth. He plans a secret base in the "Triton" system, next to an unstable star that could go supernova. Pierrot, Psyche and Metro are captured while investigating and escape with a smuggler. The real science is limited to the Sun's place in the galaxy and remarks on stars.

## Simplified but fine
- "The outer edge of the galaxy, the outer region where the solar system is located": the Sun is about 26,000 ly from the centre of a disc about 50,000 ly in radius, so "outer region" is loose but fair.
- A system with a red giant and a white dwarf, an unstable star that "might explode at any moment in a supernova", and too much carbon dioxide raising the pulse rate are all plausible.
- The Earth film ("30 billionth inhabitant ... born in Bombay", "40 tons [of explosives] per person", a 176-hour traffic jam) is deliberate satirical exaggeration, not offered as fact. For comparison, the 1980s figure for nuclear arsenals was about 3–4 tonnes of TNT equivalent per person.
