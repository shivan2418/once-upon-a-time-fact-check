# Once Upon a Time... Space — s01e22: An Hostile World
Pierrot, Giglio, Sylvia and Metro cross the planet Apis, which is full of reptile-like creatures, and learn that its people descend from Earth colonists who crash-landed five centuries ago. A mad scientist's robots later drove them into the jungle. Bandits capture them and take them to the smuggler Murdoch, whom Pierrot persuades to fly him to Yama. The only real science is Metro's comparison of the planet's fauna to Earth's Mesozoic era and its prehistoric reptiles.

## Simplified but fine
- "Mesozoic era, I might say, secondary phase. The Jurassic period, Cretaceous": correct. The Mesozoic was traditionally called the Secondary era (French *ère secondaire*), and the Jurassic and Cretaceous are two of its three periods.
- "a Scutasaurus or a Tribectrachus ... well behind his time": *Scutosaurus* is a real armoured reptile from the Late Permian (about 259–252 million years ago), older than the Mesozoic, so "behind his time" fits. "Tribectrachus" is probably a mishearing, possibly *Trimerorhachis*, a Permian amphibian.
