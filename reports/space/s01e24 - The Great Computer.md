# Once Upon a Time... Space — s01e24: The Great Computer
Pierrot and Metro find Psyche and learn that the whole robot city of Yama is a spaceship in flight. Colonel Pierre and Maestro meet the Great Computer. It explains that a scientist built it on Earth three centuries ago to govern mankind, and it demands that Omega submit to its rule. The episode is a debate about machines, logic and human nature, and its only real science is a remark about the number of stars in our galaxy and a few claims about what makes humans unique.

## Common belief at the time
- **Quote:** "Only man can laugh, sing, do what he will for fun, not out of necessity."
  **Today:** Great apes laugh during tickling and rough play (Darwin, 1872; van Hooff, 1972), many birds and whales sing, and play for its own sake is widespread among mammals and birds. Fagen's *Animal Play Behavior* (1981) reviewed this in detail. These traits are not unique to humans, though human music and humour are far more elaborate.
  **Why it was believed:** "Only man laughs" and "only man plays" were old philosophical commonplaces, repeated in popular writing long after zoologists had described laughter and play in animals.

## Simplified but fine
- "There are 200 billion in our galaxy alone" (suns): the Milky Way has about 100–400 billion stars (short-scale billion); 1982 books usually gave 100–200 billion.
- "Millions of years ago you humanity came out of your caves": the "caveman" image is loose (most early humans did not live in caves, and *Homo sapiens* is about 300,000 years old), but the genus *Homo* goes back about 2.8 million years.
- A Mozart sonata and a Beethoven quartet: both composers did write in these forms.
