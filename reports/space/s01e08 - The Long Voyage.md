# Once Upon a Time... Space — s01e08: The Long Voyage
Three explorers who left Earth in 2023 in suspended animation wake up after about a thousand years and reach the planet Omega. Humans settled it centuries ago, and ships with hyperspace drives now make the trip in days. The old professor is shown Omega's asteroid mining and hydrogen tankers, and Pierrot chases a hijacked mineral train and nearly gets lost in space during a repair. Almost everything is invented future technology. The real science is limited to how stars form from hydrogen and dust, ion engines, plotting orbits around the gravity of large bodies, and matter–antimatter annihilation.

## Simplified but fine
- Hydrogen "combined with dust particles from earlier stellar explosions, it gravitates towards a center eventually to form new stars": correct in outline. "Millions of centuries" (100+ million years) overstates it: a star like the Sun takes roughly 1–50 million years to go from collapsing cloud to stable star.
- Early ships following calculated orbits around the "gravitation" of large bodies, and the ion engine as an efficient drive: both real (ion thrusters flew from 1964).
- "Anti-matter supposes anti-time" loosely echoes the Feynman–Stückelberg idea of antiparticles as particles moving backwards in time. Particle–antiparticle collisions do annihilate into energy, but the idea that this could make "the universe, time, space" disappear is the show's fiction.
- "As a famous author said, that is the question": Shakespeare, *Hamlet*.
- "Forty generations" in a thousand years works out to 25 years a generation, a reasonable figure.
