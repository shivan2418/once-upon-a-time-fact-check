# Once Upon a Time... Life — s01e10: The Neurones

The episode shows nerve cells as busy offices that receive thousands of messages, "vote" on a response and send runners along nerves. It walks through a cough reflex during a run, the sympathetic nerves speeding up the heart, and ends by saying that thought and personality come from brain chemistry.

## Outdated (since 1987)

- **Quote:** "they're the cells in the human body that are never renewed"
  **Today:** Most neurons do last a lifetime, but adults do grow some new neurons, at least in the hippocampus (a memory area). This was first shown in humans in 1998 (Eriksson and colleagues) and backed up by later work, including a 2025 study in *Science*. How much it happens is still debated.
  **Confidence:** medium

None found.

## Wrong (even in 1987)

- **Quote:** "Coughing is a reflex, so you're entitled to go straight to the command centres bypassing the brain ... Shortcut along reflex road, and straight to the spinal column."
  **Today:** The cough reflex is run by the brainstem (the medulla), which is part of the brain. It is not a spinal reflex like the knee-jerk. The episode is right that the thinking part of the brain is skipped, but wrong that the signal goes straight to the spinal cord.
  **Confidence:** medium

## Simplified but fine

- "a hundred thousand neurons [in a] pinhead" is roughly the right order of magnitude for brain tissue.
- Neurons "voting" on a response is a fair picture of how a neuron adds up its incoming signals.
- "The cell continuously receives about 30,000 pieces of information": a single neuron can have thousands to tens of thousands of synapses, so this is a reasonable figure.
- "most of our errands only take one hundredth of a second": a fair figure for nerve signals over short distances.
- Calling the mind "the product of some chemistry ... of glucose and amino acids" is a simplification, but not wrong.
