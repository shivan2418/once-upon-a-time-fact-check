# Once Upon a Time... Life — s01e22: The Vaccination

Tetanus bacteria survive in soil as spores and get into the body through a scratch while two boys move a rose bush. In vaccinated Peter, ready-made antibodies destroy the toxin at once. Unvaccinated Jumbo develops a stiff jaw and needs hospital treatment with anti-tetanus serum and antibiotics. The message: get vaccinated and keep up your boosters.

## Wrong (even in 1987)

- **Quote:** "Those are the antibodies of an antibiotic, a medicine made to fight microbes."
  **Correct today:** Antibiotics are drugs that kill bacteria or stop them from growing. They contain no antibodies. Antibodies are proteins made by the immune system, and here they came from the anti-tetanus serum. The episode mixes up the two words. (likely a translation/dubbing slip)
  **Confidence:** Medium (it may be a dubbing slip, but it is stated as fact)

## Simplified but fine

- Tetanus spores living in soil for years and entering through a small cut: correct.
- The toxin attacking nerves, and the first sign being a stiff, clenched jaw: correct (this is called "lockjaw," or trismus).
- Treatment with "purified anti-tetanus serum" plus antibiotics: correct in outline. Today the serum is usually human tetanus immune globulin rather than serum from animals, but the idea is the same.
- "you're quite safe, as long as you don't forget your boosters": correct.
- Missing one school shot leaving a child completely unprotected: unlikely in practice, since most children are vaccinated as babies, but the episode never states anything false about it.
