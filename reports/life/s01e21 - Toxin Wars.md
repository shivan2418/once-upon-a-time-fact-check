# Once Upon a Time... Life — s01e21: Toxin Wars

Air pollutants such as smoke particles get past the lungs' defenses and into a cell. The cell's nucleus makes "detoxifying enzymes" from a DNA copy (messenger RNA) to destroy them. A chemical damages a chromosome, the episode explains that such mutations can lead to cancer, and repair enzymes fix the DNA. Lysosomes and the kidneys then clear away the waste.

## Common belief at the time

- **Quote:** "But why do we need iron in proteins? / Because the iron gives them energy. / Go and order some from the spleen, the great iron reserves."
  **Correct today:** The iron in detox enzymes (the cytochrome P450 family) is part of the machinery that grabs oxygen and drives the chemical reaction. It is not a source of energy. The body's main iron store is the liver, with more held in bone-marrow and spleen cells. The spleen recycles iron from old red blood cells, but it isn't "the great iron reserve."
  **Why it was believed:** Because the spleen breaks down old red cells and recovers their iron, older textbooks and popular references often called it the body's iron store (the line about iron "giving energy" was never standard, but the item is filed here for the spleen claim).
  **Confidence:** Medium-high
  **Severity:** minor

## Simplified but fine

- "every cell has special agents called detoxifying enzymes": most detox work happens in the liver, but many cells (in the lungs, for example) do have these enzymes.
- The detox enzymes are shown purely as protectors. In reality, the same enzymes that process smoke chemicals like benzopyrene can turn them into the form that damages DNA. Scientists knew this by 1987, but the episode never claims otherwise, so it's left out rather than counted as an error.
- "Chromosome 6" for the detox genes and "chromosome 4" for the damage: these numbers look arbitrary (the main smoke-processing gene, CYP1A1, sits on chromosome 15), and the dialogue may be mistranscribed. Not counted.
- "DNA ... must never ever leave the cell nucleus", with an RNA copy carried out instead: correct.
- Mutation leading to cells that multiply into cancer, and DNA repair enzymes: correct in outline.
- Kidneys filtering "more than a litre of blood" each minute: right for both kidneys together.
